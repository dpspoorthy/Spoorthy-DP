— our story —

A little cafe with a lot of heart

Dora Cafe began as a single espresso machine and a big dream: a place where the smell of fresh pastries greets you at the door and the chairs are worn soft from years of long conversations. We roast small batches, bake daily, and keep every table warm for whoever walks in.

Whether you're here for a quiet solo morning with a book or a lively catch-up with friends, our cozy corners and warm lighting are made for lingering a little longer.
